## Requirements for ngkit

- **StateBlock**: tones `neutral`, `success`, `warning` and `danger`, with a
  `live` input (`off`, `polite` or `assertive`).
- **EmptyState** and **PageSkeleton**, for list and page loading.
- **StatusBadge**: a short label with a tone, plus a compact dot variant for
  table cells.
- **Toast** (new): transient messages from a root service, stacked bottom right,
  with an optional action ("Undo") and `role="status"`.
- **ProgressBar** (new): determinate (`done` of `total`) and indeterminate, with
  a compact variant for the bottom bar.
