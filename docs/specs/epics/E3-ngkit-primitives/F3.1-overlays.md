## Requirements for ngkit

- **One overlay owns dismissal**: backdrop click, Esc, focus trap and return,
  and scroll lock, on `@angular/cdk/dialog`.
- **Sizes**: `dialog` (centred) and `side-panel` (a right drawer, full height,
  width set by a token, 480 px by default). The media editor
  ([F4.1](../E4-shared-domain-components/F4.1-media-editor.md)) is a side
  panel.
- **Dirty guard**: a `dirty` signal input. Dismissing while dirty opens a
  confirm ("Discard changes?") instead of closing, and only an explicit choice
  discards.
- **Stacking**: a confirm opened from a side panel stacks above it and returns
  focus to it.
- **Slots**: header, body and actions.
- **ConfirmDialog**: a title, a message or projected body, and confirm and
  cancel labels, with a `danger` tone. It resolves to a boolean.
