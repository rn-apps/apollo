The Artists, Genres and Composers pages: counted values with a drill-down into
the tracks that carry one. API: `GET /v1/facets/{field}` and `GET /v1/tracks`
filtered by the chosen value. Multi-value fields follow the plural vocabulary of
[ADR-0016](../../../adr/ADR-0016.md). Both the list and the table variants come
from the one E4 facet view.
