Editing album-level tags (album, album artist, year, disc count and similar)
once for every track of an album. API: `PATCH /v1/albums/{id}`, specified in old
apollo but never routed. It is a bulk edit over the album's tracks through the
E4 bulk edit panel and dry-run flow, with album artwork handled by
[F7.2](../E7-artwork-and-annotations/F7.2-album-artwork.md).
