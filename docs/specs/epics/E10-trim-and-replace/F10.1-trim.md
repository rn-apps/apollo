Trimming a track or a staged file to a start and end time, previewed and then
applied as a `trim` job with an audit entry. API: `POST /v1/tracks/{id}/trim`
and `POST /v1/inbox/trim`. The trim panel is part of the E4 media editor and the
time formatting comes from the shared formatters.
