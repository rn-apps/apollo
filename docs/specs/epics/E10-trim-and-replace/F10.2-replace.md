Replacing a track's audio file from a local path or a URL. The candidate is
probed and judged by the quality gate (codec, bitrate, duration against the
current file) before anything changes; the swap is a `replace` job with an audit
entry that undo can reverse. API: `POST /v1/tracks/{id}/replace`. The replace
panel is part of the E4 media editor, not an inline flow of its own.
