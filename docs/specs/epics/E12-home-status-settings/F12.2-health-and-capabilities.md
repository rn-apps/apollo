Reporting whether the library, index, Music.app, yt-dlp, ffmpeg and the worker
are usable, and which features are therefore enabled (for example `artwork_url`
disabled under iCloud Sync Library). API: `GET /v1/health`,
`POST /v1/health/refresh` and `GET /v1/capabilities`. The frontend reads
capabilities to show an action disabled with a reason instead of hiding it.
