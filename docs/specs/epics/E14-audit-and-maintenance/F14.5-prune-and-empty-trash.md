New work. Pruning audit entries by age and permanently emptying the trash where
removed files wait for undo, per [ADR-0009](../../../adr/ADR-0009.md). It adds
prune and empty-trash actions to the maintenance endpoints under `/v1`, each
previewed through the E4 dry-run flow, and refuses to prune what an undo still
needs.
