One audit entry per operation (`tag_write`, `file_move`, `file_replace`,
`item_add`, `item_remove`, `inbox_promote`, `export`, `playlist_write`), grouped
into batches, each carrying what undo needs. API: `GET /v1/audit` and
`GET /v1/audit/batches`. Every mutating feature writes through this one log.
