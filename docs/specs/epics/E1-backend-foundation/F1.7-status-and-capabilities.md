What the app can do on this machine, and the status payload the shell shows.

## Design

`media/tools.py` probes `ffmpeg`, `ffprobe`, `yt-dlp` and `osascript` through
`Process.execute`, and detects whether iCloud Sync Library is active. It runs
once at startup and again on `POST /v1/status/refresh`.

Capabilities, each `{available, reason}`, are: `trim`, `replace`, `download`,
`music_app`, `artwork_url` and `library_mutation`. iCloud Sync Library turns off
`library_mutation` and `artwork_url`.

`GET /v1/status` returns `version`, `library_root`, `tracks`, `errors`,
`worker`, `tools` and `capabilities`. Its `tracks` and `errors` counts are
served from snapshots. The status frame of the event stream carries the same
payload.

The kit's `/readyz` has two required checks, `apollo-db` and `library-actor`.
