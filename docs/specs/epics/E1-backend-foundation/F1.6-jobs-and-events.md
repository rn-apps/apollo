`platform/jobs.py` and `platform/events.py`: the in-process worker of
[ADR-0007](../../../adr/ADR-0007.md), and the one stream of
[ADR-0008](../../../adr/ADR-0008.md).

## Design

- `JobType` is a `StrEnum`, stored as `VARCHAR`. Each registered handler
  declares `mutates_library: bool`.
- `JobWorker(sessions, handlers, max_workers=4)` is an asyncio task. It claims
  `pending` jobs up to its free capacity, runs each handler as a task, wakes
  on `notify()` or every 2 s, and drains within a shutdown grace (10 s).
- `JobContext` provides `progress(done, total, message)`, `log(level, text)` and
  `checkpoint()`. `checkpoint()` raises `JobCancelled` once a cancel is
  requested, and re-reads the flag at most twice a second.
- **Startup.** Rows still `running` become `failed` ("interrupted by restart").
  If any rows are still `pending`, the worker starts *held*.
  `POST /v1/jobs/worker/resume` releases them. Jobs submitted after startup
  are claimed even while the worker is held.
- **Terminal transition.** A `mutates_library` job calls the snapshot registry's
  `invalidate_all()`, then history is pruned to `APOLLO_JOB_HISTORY_LIMIT`.
- `GET /v1/events` is `sse_response` over a generator that ticks every 500 ms.
  Each tick emits `job` frames for changed non-terminal jobs, and a `status`
  frame when its payload changes. On connect it emits every non-terminal job
  and seeds terminal ones silently. The `id:` is a monotonic tick number.

Routes: `POST /v1/jobs` (202, `{jobId}`), `GET /v1/jobs` (cursor-paged),
`GET /v1/jobs/{id}` (with log), `POST /v1/jobs/{id}/cancel`,
`GET /v1/jobs/worker`, and `POST /v1/jobs/worker/{pause|resume}`.

## S1.6.1 — Worker claims, runs and finishes jobs

**State:** New

**Acceptance:**

- `test_s1_6_1_1_capacity`: with `max_workers=2` and five slow jobs, never more
  than two are `running`, and a job is never marked `running` before its
  handler starts.
- `test_s1_6_1_2_notify_wakes`: a submitted job starts within 100 ms, without
  waiting for the 2 s poll.
- `test_s1_6_1_3_progress_and_log_persist`: progress and log lines written by a
  handler are in `job` and `job_log` while it runs.
- `test_s1_6_1_4_failure_recorded`: a handler exception gives `failed`, with the
  message, and the worker keeps running.
- `test_s1_6_1_5_invalidates_on_mutation`: a `mutates_library` job's terminal
  transition invalidates snapshots, and a non-mutating one does not.

## S1.6.2 — Cancel, restart recovery and held leftovers

**State:** New

**Acceptance:**

- `test_s1_6_2_1_cooperative_cancel`: cancelling a running job makes its next
  `checkpoint()` raise. The job ends `cancelled`.
- `test_s1_6_2_2_restart_marks_interrupted`: a `running` row from a previous run
  becomes `failed` "interrupted by restart" at startup.
- `test_s1_6_2_3_leftovers_held`: `pending` rows from a previous run are not
  claimed until resume. A job submitted after startup is claimed at once.
- `test_s1_6_2_4_bounded_shutdown`: shutdown with a job that ignores
  cancellation returns within the grace period, and the job is left for
  restart recovery.
- `test_s1_6_2_5_history_pruned`: terminal history is pruned to the limit.

## S1.6.3 — Job routes

**State:** New

**Acceptance:**

- `test_s1_6_3_1_submit_and_get`: `POST /v1/jobs` with a registered type returns
  202 and a `jobId`, and `GET /v1/jobs/{id}` shows it with its log. An unknown
  type gets a `validation` problem.
- `test_s1_6_3_2_worker_state`: `GET /v1/jobs/worker` reports `held`, `paused`
  or `running`, and the held count. Pause and resume change it.

## S1.6.4 — Event stream

**State:** New

**Acceptance:**

- `test_s1_6_4_1_connect_seeds`: a client that connects with one running job and
  one finished job receives one `job` frame and one `status` frame.
- `test_s1_6_4_2_progress_frames`: a job's progress changes produce `job`
  frames, and an unchanged tick produces none.
- `test_s1_6_4_3_no_replay`: reconnecting with `Last-Event-ID` gives the same
  frames as a fresh connect.
