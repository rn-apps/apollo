Showing, replacing and clearing artwork on a staged inbox file, addressed by
path ([ADR-0018](../../../adr/ADR-0018.md)). API: `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE`
`/v1/inbox/artwork`, through the one artwork endpoint contract of
[ADR-0011](../../../adr/ADR-0011.md). The inbox target of the E4 media editor
supplies the adapter; no second picker exists.
