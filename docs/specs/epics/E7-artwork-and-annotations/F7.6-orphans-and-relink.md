Finding annotations whose artist or album no longer exists in the index and
pointing them at the right one. API: `GET /v1/annotations/orphans` and
`POST /v1/annotations/relink` (dry run, then apply, merging on collision). The
page is the settings annotations view and uses the E4 dry-run flow.
