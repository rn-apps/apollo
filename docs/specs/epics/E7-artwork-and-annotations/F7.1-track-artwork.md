Showing, replacing and clearing a track's embedded artwork. API:
`GET /v1/tracks/{id}/artwork` (ETag, thumbnails cached under `.apollo/cache`),
`PUT /v1/tracks/{id}/artwork` (file or URL) and
`DELETE /v1/tracks/{id}/artwork`. URL fetch is a capability that is disabled
when iCloud Sync Library is active. The UI is the E4 artwork picker inside the
E4 media editor.
