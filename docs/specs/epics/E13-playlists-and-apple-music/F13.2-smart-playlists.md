Playlists defined by a query that re-evaluates against the index, using the
search of [F5.2](../E5-library-browse/F5.2-search-and-fields.md). They share the
`/v1/playlists` endpoints of F13.1 with a `smart` type and a stored query. Saved
queries elsewhere stay transient ([ADR-0015](../../../adr/ADR-0015.md)); only a
smart playlist persists one.
