Creating, listing, renaming, deleting and filling static playlists. API:
`GET /v1/playlists`, `POST /v1/playlists`, `GET /v1/playlists/{id}`,
`PATCH /v1/playlists/{id}` and `DELETE /v1/playlists/{id}`. The detail page is
the E4 track workspace with its inspector and selection toolbar, so a playlist
track opens in the shared media editor. `from-search` is deprecated and not
rebuilt.
