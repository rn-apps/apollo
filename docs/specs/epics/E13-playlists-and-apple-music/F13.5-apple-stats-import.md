Reading play counts, ratings and loved flags from Music.app into `apollo.db`,
one way ([ADR-0017](../../../adr/ADR-0017.md)). API: `GET /v1/apple/status`,
`POST /v1/apple/import-stats` (an `apple_stats` job), `GET /v1/apple/stats` and
`GET /v1/apple/reconcile`. The stats feed the Home most played and loved lists
and the sortable play columns.
