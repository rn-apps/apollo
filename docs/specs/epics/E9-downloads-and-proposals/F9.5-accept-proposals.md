Reviewing proposed tags row by row and applying the accepted ones. API:
`POST /v1/proposals/accept`, which writes through the single-edit and bulk-edit
workflows of [E6](../E6-tag-editing/index.md) with an audit batch. The review is
the E4 dry-run flow (diff and confirm), not a separate review dialog.
